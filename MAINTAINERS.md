## Becoming a maintainer

Purser has a single maintainer today and is **actively looking to grow the
team**. Sustained, high-quality contributions and sound review judgment are the
path in; the full criteria and approval process are in
[`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md#7-becoming-and-stepping-down-as-a-maintainer). If
you are interested, start by contributing (see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md))
and say so in an issue.
